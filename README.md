# MicroTime

MicroTime helps students use the gaps between their classes instead of losing them to scrolling.

Pick how long your gap is, how much energy you have and where you are, and MicroTime gives you three ideas that fit: one of your own tasks, something different like course practice, and a proper break. Add your timetable once and it finds your gaps for you.

**Try it:** [MicroTime Lite](./) (no sign-in) · [Full version](https://claude.ai/artifact/GnSWLKQsNdcYekugYajLan) (leaderboard, quiet spots, AI flashcards)

## What it does

- **Next gap:** upload your timetable (.ics) and see your next free slot and how long it is.
- **Week planner:** add tasks of any kind (study, errands, emails, gym) with a length and due date. MicroTime fits them into your gaps, most urgent first.
- **Unfinished work moves forward:** stop a task halfway and the time left goes into your next free gap.
- **Timer:** pause, +5 minutes, a break halfway, a warning 2 minutes before the end, and a focus mode that notices when you leave the page.
- **Points, streaks and a daily challenge** to keep it going.

The full version also has a weekly leaderboard with class scores, a shared list of quiet spots on campus, flashcards made from your own notes, and quick practice exercises for your courses.

## Why we built it

We surveyed 48 students and interviewed four face to face. Most used only a few of their gaps, mainly because the gap felt too short, they were tired, or they didn't know what to do. Planners they had tried felt like a chore or broke as soon as the day changed. MicroTime tries to fix that with short, ready-made ideas and a plan that adapts.

## How it works

Everything is one file, `index.html`, with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Your tasks, timetable and points are saved in your own browser (localStorage), so nothing leaves your device in the Lite version.
